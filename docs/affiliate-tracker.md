## Application readiness

Before applying, publish the real domain, working tools, substantive original descriptions, accurate Privacy and Terms pages, affiliate disclosure, and a real contact method. Do not claim traffic, testing, or product experience that Free Toolkit does not have.

Recommended order: apply to PartnerStack for closely relevant SaaS/developer offers; then review Awin, CJ, or impact.com for specific PDF, e-signature, productivity, and hosting advertisers. Apply to Amazon India only when there are genuine India-relevant product recommendations; add the US marketplace only when there is a meaningful US audience. Consider vCommission and Cuelinks for actual India-focused campaigns and Admitad when a listed advertiser is a clear fit.

Before enabling an offer, record approval and terms, create the approved tracking destination in the network, configure the matching `NEXT_PUBLIC_AFFILIATE_*_URL`, verify the link and destination, and ensure the nearby disclosure is visible. Recheck network terms periodically. Official program details can change.
